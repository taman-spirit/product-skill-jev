# 1. Executive Summary

AI PM Skills is an AI co-pilot for Product Managers that automates the most time-consuming parts of the PM workflow. Instead of spending hours writing PRDs, calculating RICE scores, and manually tracking stakeholders, PMs describe what they need in plain language and the AI handles the rest.

The platform consists of two delivery channels (Telegram bot + web portal) and a file-based workspace where all documents are stored as plain markdown files the PM always owns.

---

# 2. The Customer Problem

## 2.1. Problem Statement

Product Managers spend 60-70% of their time on process overhead: writing boilerplate documents, filling in templates, tracking what changed, chasing approvals. This time should be spent on actual product decisions.

## 2.2. Customer Evidence

### 2.2.1. PM Time Study

Internal survey of 20 PMs shows:
- Average 4 hours to write a complete PRD from scratch
- Average 45 minutes to calculate and document RICE scores
- 35% of PRDs become outdated within 2 weeks without a versioning system

### 2.2.2. Competitive Gap

Existing tools (Notion, Jira, Confluence) provide templates but no intelligence. The PM still does all the thinking and writing. No tool on the market combines AI writing assistance with structured PM methodology enforcement.

## 5.2. Epic Detail

### EP-001 - Telegram Bot

**Module:** Bot | **Priority:** P0 | **Version:** v1.0 | **Status:** approved | **Points:** 21

Enables Product Managers to interact with the AI PM co-pilot via Telegram, capturing feature requests, running RICE scoring, and getting project status updates from any device.

**Key Acceptance Criteria:**
- PM can send a message describing a feature and receive a structured FR document
- RICE scoring completes in under 5 messages
- Bot responds within 10 seconds for simple commands

**Out of scope for this Epic:** Multi-user collaboration, message scheduling
